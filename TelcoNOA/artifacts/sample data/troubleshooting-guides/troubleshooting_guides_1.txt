
# Troubleshooting Steps

### TSG sources consulted:
0. [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md)
1. [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)
2. [SONiC - Critical Process Checker - StoppedCriticalServiceOrProcessDetected](sonic-critical-process-checker-stopped-critical-process.md)
3. [SONiC - Critical Process Checker - UnhealthyCriticalProcessDetected](sonic-critical-process-checker-unhealthy-critical-service-or-process.md)
4. [SONiC - Critical Process Checker - CriticalProcessHealthChecker failed](sonic-critical-process-health-checker-failed.md)

### Possible causes

1. **syncd Process Crash**
   - The JSON payload indicates that the `syncd` process exited unexpectedly with an exit status of 2. This is consistent with a process crash.
   - The syslog entries in the source documents show similar patterns of `syncd` crashing due to assertion failures.
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

2. **Assertion Failure in sbusdma.c**
   - The syslog entries in the source documents indicate that an assertion failure in `sbusdma.c` is a common cause for `syncd` crashes.
   - The error message in the syslog (`ERROR: Assertion failed: ((ch >= 0) && (ch < soc->max_sbusdma_channels))`) matches the pattern described in the source documents.
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

### Possible mitigation steps

1. **Check if syncd Process is Running**
   - Use the following command to check if the `syncd` process is running:
     ```bash
     pgrep -fx "/usr/bin/dsserve /usr/bin/syncd --diag -u -p /etc/sai.d/sai.profile"
     ```
   - If the command ends without a number following it, then the process `syncd` is not running.
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

2. **Check Syslog for Cause of Failure**
   - Examine the syslog files (`/var/log/syslog` or `/var/log/syslog.1`) for lines indicating the cause of the crash:
     ```bash
     grep "syncd" /var/log/syslog*
     ```
   - Look for lines containing `ERROR: Assertion failed`.
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

3. **Isolate the Device from the Network**
   - Follow the isolation steps to prevent further impact on the network:
     - For TOR devices: [TOR Isolation](https://phynet.trafficmanager.net/UnifiedDeviceAccess/Isolate/Tor)
     - For other devices: [SONiC Isolate, Unisolate Device](~/wiki/tsg/sonic-tsg/sonic-isolate-unisolate-device.md)
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

4. **Restart the SwSS Service**
   - Restart the SwSS service and wait for 1 minute:
     ```bash
     sudo systemctl restart swss
     ```
   - Warning: Restarting the SwSS service will impact the state of the device.
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

5. **Restart the DHCP Relay Service (if applicable)**
   - If the device is a T0 device running 20180330.XXX firmware, restart the DHCP relay service:
     ```bash
     sudo systemctl restart dhcp_relay
     ```
   - Warning: Restarting the DHCP relay service will impact the state of the device.
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

6. **Unisolate the Device from the Network**
   - Follow the unisolation steps to bring the device back into the network:
     - [SONiC Isolate, Unisolate Device](~/wiki/tsg/sonic-tsg/sonic-isolate-unisolate-device.md)
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

7. **Verify Device Health**
   - Check the device health using the following TSGs:
     - [SONiC Check for Packet Loss](~/wiki/tsg/sonic-tsg/sonic-check-for-packet-loss.md)
     - [SONiC Check BGP Health](~/wiki/tsg/sonic-tsg/sonic-bgp-check-bgp-health.md)
     - [SONiC Check LAG Health](~/wiki/tsg/sonic-tsg/sonic-check-lag-health.md)
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)

8. **Contact SONiC Team**
   - Since this issue is actively tracked by the SONiC team, send an email to **sonicdev@microsoft.com** including the ticket link.
   - Relevant source: [SONiC syncd Assertion Failed](sonic-syncd-assertion-failed.md), [SONiC syncd crash due to sbusdma assertion failure](sonic-syncd-crash-due-to-sbusdma-assertion-failure.md)
